References

This course draws on peer-reviewed research, industry standards, and technical documentation published between 2015-2025. All citations follow APA 7th edition format.

BibTeX Database​

Complete bibliographic entries are maintained in static/references.bib for automated citation management.

Reference Categories​

ROS 2 and Navigation (10-15 sources)​

Macenski, S., Martín, F., White, R., & Clavero, J. G. (2020). The Marathon 2: A navigation system. In 2020 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) (pp. 2718-2725). IEEE. https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS45743.2020.9341207

Additional references covering ROS 2 architecture, Nav2, DDS, behavior trees, and SLAM will be added during research tasks (T011-T016).

Simulation Environments (8-10 sources)​

References for Gazebo architecture, URDF/SDF modeling, Unity Robotics Hub, physics engines, and sensor simulation will be added during research tasks.

NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Synthetic Data (5-8 sources)​

References for Omniverse, USD, domain randomization, PhysX, and sim-to-real transfer will be added during research tasks.

Vision-Language-Action Models (10-15 sources)​

References for RT-1, RT-2, OpenVLA, transformer architectures, imitation learning, and multimodal AI will be added during research tasks.

Humanoid Robotics Education (10-15 sources)​

References for bipedal locomotion, humanoid platforms (Unitree, Boston Dynamics, Agility Robotics), manipulation, and human-robot interaction will be added during research tasks.

Curriculum Design and ABET (5-10 sources)​

References for engineering education, learning outcomes assessment, accreditation standards, and pedagogical approaches will be added during research tasks.

Lab Infrastructure and ROI (5-10 sources)​

References for educational ROI analysis, lab equipment procurement, safety standards, and workforce development will be added during research tasks.

Research Database Access​

Students and educators are encouraged to access original sources through:

  • IEEE Xplore: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org (robotics conferences ICRA, IROS, Humanoids)
  • ACM Digital Library: https://dl.acm.org (HRI, autonomous agents)
  • arXiv: https://arxiv.org (preprints for RT-1, RT-2, OpenVLA)
  • ROS Documentation: https://docs.ros.org
  • NVIDIA Technical Docs: https://docs.nvidia.com/isaac

Citation Policy​

All technical claims in this course are supported by:

  • Peer-reviewed publications (journals, conferences)
  • Industry-standard technical documentation
  • Open-source project repositories

When referencing this course in academic work, cite as:

Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course Contributors. (2025).
Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course Specification.
https://your-organization.github.io/physical-ai-course/

Contributing References​

Found a relevant source not listed here? Submit via:

  1. Fork the course repository
  2. Add BibTeX entry to static/references.bib
  3. Open pull request with justification

All contributions reviewed by course maintainers for relevance and quality.
